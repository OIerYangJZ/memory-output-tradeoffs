\subsection{Memory-capped constructions}
\label{sec:w1-upper}

\begin{proposition}[Block hybrid]
\label{prop:w1-block-hybrid}
Fix $p,h\geq1$ and let $q=\min\{m,ph\}$.  There is a deterministic
zero-error $p$-pass compiler for \cref{def:w1-dispersed-stream} with
$K=Q$ that
aggregates $q$ coordinates and passes the other update records through in
the public $p$-block hybrid mode of $E_{\mathrm{rel}}$.  It obeys
\begin{align}
 S&\leq \left\lceil\frac qp\right\rceil\log_2Q
       +O(\log(mQrp)),
 \label{eq:w1-upper-state}\\
 A_p&\leq(r-1)(m-q)\log_2Q+O(r\log(mQrp)),
 \label{eq:w1-upper-A}\\
 B_{\mathrm{rel}}^{\mathrm{out}}
 &\leq q\log_2Q+r(m-q)\log_2Q\notag\\
 &\quad+O((p+r)\log(mQrp)).
 \label{eq:w1-upper-output}
\end{align}
In the compact-output corner $h=\lceil m/p\rceil$, this becomes
\begin{align}
 B_{\mathrm{rel}}^{\mathrm{out}}
 &\leq m\log_2Q+O(p\log(mQrp)),\\
 S&\leq\left\lceil\frac mp\right\rceil\log_2Q
       +O(\log(mQrp)).
 \label{eq:w1-compact-upper}
\end{align}
In the compact-output corner $q=m$ for any number of rounds, and for
every $q$ when $r=2$, the construction satisfies
$A_p+pS\leq m\log_2Q+O((p+r)\log(mQrp))$, and the same bound holds for
$A_p+\Sigma^{\to}_p$.  Compared with
\eqref{eq:w1-pass-lower} and \eqref{eq:w1-frontier-box} at $\delta=0$,
whose right-hand side is $m\log_2Q-\lceil\log_2(p+1)\rceil$, it is
therefore optimal to leading order whenever $p\log(mQrp)=o(m\log Q)$; the
additive terms are not claimed to be optimal, and for $p$ comparable to $m$
they dominate.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Partition the first $q$ coordinates into at most $p$ consecutive blocks of
size at most $h$.  Each pass is re-delivered by the input source
(\cref{def:w1-pass-accounting}), so the compiler keeps no copy of the
stream between passes.  On pass $t$, keep only the residues of block $t$ and add
their first $r-1$ rounds modulo $Q$; when the final-round mates arrive,
emit the corresponding aggregate base-$Q$ block.  The partial sums are held
as one packed integer in $\mathsf{ser}_{\mathrm{store}}$ at its literal bit
length (\cref{def:compiler}); the live packed block, indices, and framing
give \eqref{eq:w1-upper-state} at the crossings.  Each block is packed
separately, which
costs at most one bit per block; together with the per-block framing this is
the $O(p\log(mQrp))$ term in the output bound.  On the first pass,
emit every share of each unselected coordinate in the declared round-major
order, packing the residues of each round in base $Q$ and flushing them
at the end of that round, so that the residues of round $r-1$ are written
before the crossing; these rotations already have the correct product.  The selected coordinates can be balanced
across the $p$ blocks, so the maximum block has size $\lceil q/p\rceil$.
Their consecutive boundaries are a public function of $(m,p,q)$, so the
header stores those integers rather than $p$ separate delimiters.
The prefix contains $r-1$ residues and the
whole output contains $r$ residues for each such coordinate, giving
\eqref{eq:w1-upper-A} and \eqref{eq:w1-upper-output}.  If $ph\geq m$ there
are no passthrough coordinates, $A_p$ is the header, and
$p\lceil m/p\rceil\log_2Q\leq m\log_2Q+p\log_2Q$ gives the stated bound on
$A_p+pS$ for any $r$.  For $r=2$ and general $q$, $(m-q)\log_2Q+p\lceil
q/p\rceil\log_2Q$ is at most $m\log_2Q+p\log_2Q$, which gives the same
bound; since every forward snapshot is bounded by the same
block, the bound also holds for $A_p+\Sigma^{\to}_p$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Semantic and randomized upper corners]
\label{prop:w1-semantic-upper}
Under the same public dictionary, fix a block length $b\geq1$.  A
one-pass semantic compiler receiving $x$ emits the aggregate-mode code
with $b$-digit blocks and satisfies
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 B_{\mathrm{rel}}^{\mathrm{out}}
 &\leq m\log_2K+\lceil m/b\rceil+O(\log(mQ)),\\
 S&\leq b\log_2K+O(\log(mQ)),
\end{aligned}
 \label{eq:w1-semantic-upper}
\end{equation}
and implements the same $U_x$ as the flat stream.  With
$b=\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil$ the state is
$O(\log m\cdot\log Q)$ and the output is $m\log_2K\,(1+o(1))$.  Blocking is
needed: when $K=2^eK_{\mathrm{odd}}$ with $K_{\mathrm{odd}}>1$, a write-once
streaming writer of the single integer $\sum_jx_jK^{j-1}$ in binary,
least significant bit first, reading $x_1,x_2,\ldots$ in order, must hold
at least $\lfloor m/2\rfloor\log_2K_{\mathrm{odd}}-1$ bits after half the
digits.  For any $0\leq\delta<1/2$, an input-independent abort coin that
runs this exact compiler with probability $1-\delta$ and otherwise emits a
fixed short circuit has worst-input success at least $1-\delta$ and
expected relative output
\begin{equation}
 (1-\delta)\bigl(m\log_2K+\lceil m/b\rceil\bigr)+O(\log(mQ)+1).
 \label{eq:w1-random-upper}
\end{equation}
Thus the randomized family-relative output lower bound is matched to
leading order, up to framing and the binary-entropy additive term.  A
self-contained $R_Z$-circuit upper bound is
$O(\sum_j|\mathrm{supp}\,P_j|\log n+m\log Q)$ bits, which is
$O(m(\log m+\log Q))$ for constant-weight characters on $n=\mathrm{poly}(m)$
qubits, with at most $m$ rotations; no matching self-contained-bit or
gate-count claim is made.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Buffer the incoming aggregates $b$ at a time, held as one packed integer,
and write each full buffer as one $\lceil b\log_2K\rceil$-bit integer and
a final partial block of $b'<b$ digits in $\lceil b'\log_2K\rceil$ bits;
the buffer, an index, and framing are the only live state.  Each block loses less than one bit to rounding,
which gives the $\lceil m/b\rceil$ term.  For the single packed integer,
let $P=\sum_{i\leq j}x_iK^{i-1}$ be the value of the first $j$ digits and
$N=P+K^jR$ the final integer.  Since $K^jR\equiv0\pmod{2^{ej}}$ and the
higher bits of $N$ depend on $R$, a correct write-once writer has written at
most the low $ej$ bits of $P$, so at most $2^{ej+1}$ distinct written
prefixes occur.  Two digit prefixes with the same snapshot and the same
written prefix produce the same $N$ for every $R$, hence $P=P'$.  The
$K^j$ values of $P$ therefore need at least $K^j/2^{ej+1}=K_{\mathrm{odd}}^{\,j}/2$
distinct snapshots, which, being self-delimiting
(\cref{def:w1-pass-accounting}), need at least
$j\log_2K_{\mathrm{odd}}-1$ bits by Kraft's inequality; take
$j=\lfloor m/2\rfloor$.  \Cref{prop:w1-stream-equivalence} gives
semantic equality with the flat representation.  Prefix the output with the
abort-coin branch tag for the randomized construction and take
expectations.  The self-contained construction writes each nonzero
$R_{P_j}(2\pi x_j/Q)$ with its address and rational parameter.
\end{proof}
